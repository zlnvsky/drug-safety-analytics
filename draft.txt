## Data Decisions

**Age imputation**: ~161k records (40%) have missing age values.
Decision: kept as NaN to avoid assumptions.
These records are excluded from age-based and demographic analysis only.

**Weight**: ~60k records (17%) have weight data available.
Excluded from main analysis due to high missingness (83%).
Available for supplementary weight-based analysis.

**Date anomalies**: 65 records where mfr_dt > fda_dt dropped as data entry errors.
